FSChecklist Privacy Notice
Version 1.0, July 2026

FSChecklist does not create user accounts, include advertising, store voice
recordings, or send telemetry to the FSChecklist developer.

Voice recognition is provided by Windows. If the Windows online speech
recognition option is enabled, Microsoft may process speech according to the
Microsoft privacy statement and the user's Windows privacy settings.

Application settings are stored locally at:

%LocalAppData%\FSChecklist\settings.json

FSChecklist reads simulator telemetry through SimConnect when Microsoft Flight
Simulator 2024 is running. This data is processed locally and is not sent to
the FSChecklist developer.

Diagnostic information is shown locally in the application. Users choose what
information to include when they voluntarily open a support request.

Questions about this notice may be submitted at:

https://github.com/joaopedroffranco/FSChecklist/issues
